Our flagship story

Insulin, made at home.

In 2007 Popular became the first company in Bangladesh to manufacture human insulin. Our hormone and insulin unit now supplies doctors, hospitals and pharmacies across the country.

For a person with diabetes, a local maker means steady supply and a price set in taka.
